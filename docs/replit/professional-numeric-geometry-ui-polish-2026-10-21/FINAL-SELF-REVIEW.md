# Final professional self-review

## Audit opinion

The initial hypotheses were partly correct, not a reason for indiscriminate
redesign. The three-circle pattern had unstable label mapping, but its area
math was sound. Precise readings now have a complete consistent key; bars/list
remain the recommendation for precision-first comparisons. A primary hero
circle has a different, explicitly labeled hierarchy role and is retained.
There was no actual same-value circle-plus-line failure in the inspected main.

The more important field repairs concern editing and truthful actions, not
just smaller decoration: range/direction preservation, full increments, safe
arithmetic, invalid text refusal, locked states, one clear affordance and
coordinate-honest touch. Quiet actions retain full48px targets.

## Corrections made after review

1. Preserve backwards selection direction as well as selection bounds.
2. Refuse arithmetic outside safe scaled integer range; do not turn an extreme
   decimal into `NaN` or round an unsafe long integer.
3. Refuse nonempty invalid numeric syntax instead of `NaN`→5 or`12oops`→17;
   retain manual editing and consumer-owned validation.
4. Keep blocked empty input empty, including an upper bound with no valid step.
5. Separate declared aria locks from derived boundary/readonly state so
   reinitialized live clones do not become permanently unavailable.
6. Suppress the browser cancel only with a custom clear; use original touch
   coordinates to reject out-of-button retargeted clear/step/reveal actions.
7. Wait for settled focus-shadow state in tests rather than assuming150ms is
   enough under concurrent browser load.
8. Correct legacy geometry measurement to use real circle/negative marker/
   missing-slot coordinates, not independently movable text.
9. Rebuild and byte-check standalone; exercise source and standalone critical
   editing/touch paths, not only a static generator comparison.

## What the result does not claim

- No physical Android/iOS device or real touch verification.
- No Safari/WebKit/Firefox engine certification, native keyboard/native zoom,
  system text enlargement, safe-area, WebView/PWA or screen-reader certification.
- Chromium emulated touch and CSS200% text tests are named precisely; they are
  not substitutes for those platform tests.
- Stepping is safe finite decimal arithmetic, not arbitrary-precision math.
  Unrepresentable values remain editable/copyable with unavailable step actions.
- The canonical raw/display documentation is not a global parser migration;
  unknown, zero, negative and loading are not collapsed into one state.
- Accounting was actually consulted for narrow numeric-field lessons; Zaman
  was not accessible. No fabricated external design inspection is claimed.
- Tests cover representative normal/stress/state combinations and prior
  regression contracts, not every possible content/browser combination.

## Preserved boundaries

Shared identity assets/tokens are byte-unchanged; no package added. No backend,
authentication, financial validation, product flow, new screen, source family
merge, DRAFT promotion or custom icon drawing. Existing scale refusals and
resize/inert/visibility/full-pause/reduced-motion lifecycle behavior remain.
Standalone is generated, not hand-patched. Main is not edited or merged.

## Readiness standard

Ready for maintainer review means all confirmed in-scope issues have source
repairs and final regression evidence, the generated mirror matches, and an
open unmerged PR is delivered. It does **not** mean unconditional native
platform approval or authorization to merge. Final commands/results and
delivery identity are in the verification report and downloadable receipt.
